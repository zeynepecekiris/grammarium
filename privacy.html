Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeynep Ece Kırış ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Grammarium mobile app (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous account data. When you first open the App, we create an anonymous account with a random identifier. We do not ask for your name, email or password.
  • Learning progress. Lessons completed, stars, XP, streaks, level, saved words and review history, so we can sync and restore your progress.
  • Content you create. Messages you send in AI chat, custom scenarios you write, and text or speech you submit in exercises.
  • Voice input. If you use speaking exercises, the App uses your microphone (with your permission) to evaluate your speech. We do not keep audio recordings longer than needed to process them.
  • App settings. Such as your native language and subtitle language.
  • Purchase status. Whether you have an active Pro subscription. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Advertising and device data. In the free version, our advertising partners may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model and ad interactions.
  • Diagnostics. Basic crash and performance data to fix problems.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App, save your progress and restore it when you reinstall.
  • To generate AI tutor replies, feedback, translations and character voices.
  • To schedule your vocabulary reviews.
  • To manage your Pro subscription.
  • To show ads in the free version.
  • To fix bugs, keep the App secure and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the App:

  • Supabase – anonymous accounts and progress storage.
  • OpenAI – AI chat replies, feedback and text-to-speech voices. Your chat messages and lesson text are sent to OpenAI to generate responses. Please do not share sensitive personal information in AI chat.
  • Advertising partners – to show ads in the free version.
  • Apple – in-app purchases and subscriptions.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and may process it outside your country.

4. Tracking and ads

On iOS, we ask for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads may still be shown but will not be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App's settings; this permanently deletes your account and learning data from our servers. You can also contact us to request deletion.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
