Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Grammarium creates an anonymous account automatically so your progress is saved. You don't need to enter an email or password.

I reinstalled the app. Where is my progress?

If you reinstall on the same device, your progress is restored automatically. Progress is not transferred between different devices.

What does Grammarium Pro include?

Pro removes ads and unlocks all premium features. You can see the current Pro features and prices on the subscription screen in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Grammarium, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but don't see it. What should I do?

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, contact us.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and learning progress from our servers. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; please cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

The audio isn't playing.

Check that your device isn't in silent mode and the volume is up. Character voices need an internet connection; when a voice isn't available, the app uses your device's built-in voice instead.
